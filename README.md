## Featured research: Contextual Dataset Valuation

[Read the paper on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23571723) · [Code, results, and reproducibility instructions](papers/data-valuation/)

**What is a dataset worth—and to whom?** This technical note separates acquisition value, removal value, and Shapley attribution. In a controlled synthetic experiment, two specialist datasets reverse their acquisition ranking across target populations in 42 of 50 instances. Exact duplicate suppliers have zero removal value but equal Shapley attribution.

The practical contribution is a reproducible worked example and reporting protocol using established methods. It does not establish a universal price or a new valuation algorithm. Version 1.0, October 22, 2026; not peer reviewed; substantial AI assistance disclosed. Paper and archived package: CC BY 4.0.

Feedback is welcome on the assumptions, uncertainty bounds, and real-data settings that could test the limits of the demonstration.
